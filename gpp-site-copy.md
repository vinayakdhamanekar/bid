## Module 1

### Start with what care costs to deliver.

Your operational cost model, loaded as it is. Encounter lines, department cost, billed charges, service categories.

- Configured to your data shape, not the other way round

## Module 2

### Then what the health plan books.

Regulatory cost, the view the bid is built on. Uniform cost and contract rate on the same claim line.

- Both regulatory views carried on one row, switched by flag
- Contract pricing for medical groups and hospitals shown as its own step
- Cost components, schedules and roll-ups kept in your own names

## Module 3

### The cost walk.

Operations to regulatory to bid. The step most organisations cannot explain today.

- Matches encounter lines to claim lines on your keys
- Three columns on every row: in one view only, in the other only, costed differently
- Difference ledger with a reason and a method on every entry
- Residual reported, never plugged

Ask it: *Why is operational cost higher than the bid for outpatient surgery?*
Answer: the share with no claim line, the share priced by ratio, the share at contract rate, and the remainder, by plan and month.

## Give the platform a real question

Four things it answers in the first month on a new market.

1. **Tie the books.** Loaded operational and regulatory cost against your certified reports, to the cent.
2. **Name the gap.** The difference between the two views, by service category, plan and month, each driver named.
3. **Trace a figure.** Any number on any screen, back to the member-level records you provided.
4. **Prove it again.** The next month, the same rules, the restatement explained.

## How it works

**Connect.** You de-identify and upload. We never connect to your systems directly.
**Configure.** Your rules, in your names, confirmed by your subject matter expert before configuration.
**Trace.** Every figure opens to the lines behind it. Every run keeps its inputs, rules and cost run.

## What stays with you

The platform does not originate your allocation rules. It makes them repeatable, auditable and versioned.

- Bid preparation, certification and submission stay with your actuaries
- Risk scores come from your models
- Benefit design is your decision
- Trend and completion assumptions are yours, entered and kept with the run

We give you the foundation. You keep the decisions.
